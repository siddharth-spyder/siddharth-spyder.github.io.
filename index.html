Featured Research.

World Bank Data Lab · GWU Capstone · 2025
World Bank
Air Pollution as a Proxy for
Sectoral Economic Activity
A multi-country satellite econometrics study examining EDGAR-derived emission variables (NOx, SO₂, PM2.5, CH₄, BC/CO) as high-frequency GDP proxies across Pakistan, Algeria, and Myanmar — spanning Mining, Construction, and Transportation sectors. 18 notebooks, 2M+ data points.
Countries🇵🇰 Pakistan · 🇩🇿 Algeria · 🇲🇲 Myanmar
MethodsTwo-way FE · HAC OLS · ADF/KPSS · Granger · SHAP
DataEDGAR v8 · OMI NO₂ · ERA5 · ACAG PM2.5
🇵🇰
Pakistan
4 notebooks
126 districts
2019 – 2023
ADM2 panel
Satellite PM2.5 EDA — ACAG baseline across 160 ADM2 districts, longitudinal feasibility and spatial framework setup
Two-way FE (district + month) regressing PM2.5 on NO₂ and SO₂ — residuals exported as co-pollutant-independent PM2.5 signal
Roads × PM2.5 regression — motorway, primary, secondary & trunk road density OLS/WLS across 128 districts with VIF and province summaries
BC/CO as transportation proxy — district FE panel OLS, BC/CO × pop. density & wind interactions, ERA5 controls, SHAP, LMG, permutation test, Cook's distance, k-fold CV
🇩🇿
Algeria
8 notebooks
48 quarters
2011 – 2022
3 sectors
Full ETL — EDGAR filtering, monthly-to-quarterly reshape, IPCC sector classification into Mining, Construction & Transportation, merge with national accounts GDP
BC/CO ratio as transportation proxy — lagged BC/CO highly significant (p=0.000003), outperforming raw NOx across 32 quarterly obs (2011–2018)
EDA across all three sectors — GDP trends, gas distributions, correlation heatmaps, seasonality; sector modeling with Granger, ADF/KPSS, Breusch-Pagan, Ljung-Box, HAC OLS
PM2.5 residuals — log(PM2.5) ~ log(BC/CO) + log(NOx) + quarter FE, isolating combustion-independent variation for downstream use
Transportation modeling — log-log OLS, valid negative result documented (NOx adds little once temporal structure controlled)
OMI NO₂ robustness — Δlog, YoY, rolling correlation, directional agreement, GDP bridge checks vs. EDGAR NOx
🇲🇲
Myanmar
6 notebooks
49 quarters
FY2010–2023
Fiscal calendar
Data pipeline — fiscal quarter alignment (Q1=Oct–Dec), EDGAR IPCC sector mapping, merge with World Bank national accounts; Manufacturing & Transportation panels (49 obs each)
Formal modeling (RQ1 + RQ2) — log-log OLS, lag-1 features, quarter FE, HAC errors; Manufacturing → NOx/SO₂; Transportation → CO₂/PM2.5/NOx
EDA — seasonal decomposition, winsorization, correlation heatmaps across 98 total panel rows
BC/CO transportation proxy — biomass burning (3.C.1) excluded to prevent confounding, unique methodological contribution; 32 quarterly obs (FY2010-11–FY2017-18)
PM2.5 residuals — fiscal quarter FE, cointegration tested alongside ADF/KPSS; residuals feed downstream transportation modeling
OMI NO₂ validation — yearly CSV aggregation, valid-pixel coverage, Granger vs. EDGAR NOx, COVID shock comparison, GDP bridge checks
